**Description**
That pop isn't bones grinding. Pulling the joint apart drops the pressure in the joint fluid and a gas bubble forms. And studies so far haven't linked knuckle cracking to arthritis (if it hurts or swells, see a doctor).

Sources:
- Kawchuk et al., PLOS ONE (2015), real-time MRI of knuckle cracking
- Chandran Suja & Barakat, Scientific Reports 8:4600 (2018)
- deWeber et al., J Am Board Fam Med 24(2):169-174 (2011), 215 people, no link to hand osteoarthritis
- Unger, Arthritis & Rheumatism (1998): cracked only his left hand for decades (Ig Nobel 2009)
- Cleveland Clinic, Harvard Health

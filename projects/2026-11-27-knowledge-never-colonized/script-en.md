เขียนใหม่เป็นอังกฤษธรรมชาติ ไม่แปลตรง; ข้อเท็จจริงเดียวกับ research.md (ป้ายเดียวกัน)

Every country around here was colonized. Except one.
Guess the other four that were never colonized by a Western power.
Five: Bhutan. Nobody ever ruled it, though for a while it let Britain guide its foreign affairs.
Four: Nepal. It lost a war and land to Britain, but stayed independent.
Three: Iran. Occupied during the war, but never a colony.
Two: Japan. Occupied for about seven years after the war. Still not a colony.
One: Thailand. Partly thanks to reforms under King Chulalongkorn, and diplomacy that played Britain and France against each other.
But it wasn't free. Siam had to give up land: the left bank of the Mekong, and four Malay states.
And "Thai" means "free".
Which country should be on this list? Comment below.

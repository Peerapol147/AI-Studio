**Description**
Yawning because your brain needs oxygen? When people breathed 100% oxygen in an experiment, they yawned just as much 🥱 One leading idea is that yawning may help cool the brain, but scientists still debate it. (If you're yawning far more than usual for days, check with a doctor.)
